# Axpdev Lab Team

The people behind AeroFTP and what each of them looks after. Not everyone on the team is a maintainer: the roles are listed as they are.

**A.P.** ([@axpnet](https://github.com/axpnet)) - Founder, developer, maintainer, co-designer

Architecture, the Rust backend, the React frontend, the CLI and TUI, releases, and everything not listed below. AeroFTP began as a personal tool in 2024 and is still built and maintained by one person, with AI assistance that is documented openly in [AI-TRANSPARENCY.md](AI-TRANSPARENCY.md).

**E.K.** ([@EhudKirsh](https://github.com/EhudKirsh)), PhD - Researcher, quality assurance engineer, co-designer

Design across the interface and the command line: Wrappers/Overlays ([#272](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions/272) and [#276](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions/276)), the `aero` command alias ([#273](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions/273)), the table view on the Add Service page ([#274](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions/274)), the TUI ([#311](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions/311)), complex user guides ([#321](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions/321)), the Quick Connect layout ([#369](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions/369)), and [much more](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions?discussions_q=involves%3AEhudKirsh). Additionally, filed [dozens of bug reports](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/issues?q=author%3AEhudKirsh). Contributing since [#110](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/issues/110) in April 2026.

## Contact

Public discussion belongs in [issues](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/issues) and [discussions](https://github.com/axpdev-lab/aeroftp/discussions), where it stays searchable for everyone. For security reports, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening an issue.

## Contributing

Anyone can contribute without being on the team, and contributions are credited in the git history and the changelog. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), including the sign-off that every commit needs.
